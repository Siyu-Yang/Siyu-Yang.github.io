## About Me

I am a Master’s student in Biomedical Informatics at the **UTHealth Houston**, where I am honored to be advised by [Dr. Yejin Kim](https://yejinjkim.github.io). My research centers on developing advanced AI methods for biomedicine and therapeutics to accelerate scientific discovery. Currently, my work focuses on the ***de novo* design of epitope-targeted antibodies**. 

Previously, I earned an M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from the **University of Notre Dame**, where I researched **network science and computational biology** under the guidance of [Dr. Tijana Milencović](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~tmilenko/). I began my research journey at the **University of Michigan-Dearborn**, working with [Dr. Yi Lu Murphey](https://umdearborn.edu/people-um-dearborn/yi-lu-murphey) on time-series forecasting. I hold a dual degree in Computer Science (B.Eng.) and Finance (B.Econ.) from **UESTC**.
